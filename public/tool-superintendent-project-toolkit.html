Exact download contents

Good to know

  • The meeting example is a condensed action tracker, not a verbatim narrative meeting report.
  • The example's aging and overdue flags calculate against today's date, so its historical open actions will appear overdue.

Requirements, protection & printing

SoftwareDesigned for desktop Microsoft Excel. Google Sheets and Apple Numbers have not been validated.
EditingWhite cells are editable. Calculated cells are protected with no password. Use Review → Unprotect Sheet on each sheet you need to change, and unprotect before sorting a whole table or adding rows. Never sort a single column on its own.
PDFsStatic printable copies. They do not calculate and are not interactive spreadsheet replacements.
PrintingWide worksheets print on 11×17 landscape. Instructions and buyer guides use letter portrait. Print only the sheets or records you need.
