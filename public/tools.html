Before you buy

Requirements & FAQs

What is included?

Editable Excel workbooks, static printable PDFs, worked examples, a buyer guide and quick-start instructions. See each product page for the exact contents.

Can I edit the files?

Yes. White cells are editable. Calculated cells are protected with no password. To unprotect, select the worksheet, choose Review → Unprotect Sheet, and repeat for each sheet you need to edit.

Do the PDFs calculate?

No. PDFs are static printable copies of the forms and examples, not interactive spreadsheet replacements. Use the Excel workbook for calculations and dropdowns.

Which spreadsheet software is supported?

The workbooks are designed for desktop Microsoft Excel. Google Sheets and Apple Numbers have not been validated.

What paper size do they print on?

Wide worksheets use 11×17 landscape. Instructions and buyer guides use letter portrait. You can print only the sheets or records you need.

Do the logs grow automatically?

No. Capacities are fixed and listed on each product page. Unprotect the sheet before sorting a whole table or adding rows, never sort a single column on its own, and review formulas, dashboard ranges, filters and print areas if you extend a workbook.

Are the examples real projects?

The examples are adapted from historical project records. All project, contractor and staff names are fictional, and contact details and addresses are removed.
